# Draft Request Ready to Submit

On the Review step, the request shows the "Ready to submit" status, with no blocking checks remaining.

**Check:** Open the request on the **Review** step (step 4): the label in the top-right corner of the Creation Workflow bar reads "Ready to submit," and the "Submit Request" button is enabled (the message "Some checks are still unresolved." is not shown).


![Creation Workflow bar on step 4, Review: all four steps are complete and the Ready to submit label is displayed](/static/img/aka-payment-latest-requests-draft-ready.webp)

All steps are valid: the request includes expenses, the beneficiary matches the total expenses, all required supporting documents are attached, and (for reconciliation requests) reconciliation is complete.


## How to fix

- [Create a request and complete it through the Review step](../../guides/requests/how-to/create-draft.md) — After opening a new request, add expenses in the Expense Breakdown, enter payment details, and continue to the Review step.

## Used in these guides

- [Create a request and complete it through the Review step](../../guides/requests/how-to/create-draft.md) — After opening a new request, add expenses in the Expense Breakdown, enter payment details, and continue to the Review step.
- [Submit a request](../../guides/requests/how-to/submit.md) — On the Review step, click Submit Request; the system opens the first approval step and locks the request.

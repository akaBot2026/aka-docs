# Choose how to open the assistant in Preferences

In Personal Profile, on the Preferences tab, choose By organization, Right panel, or Floating bubble under AI Assistant. This setting is saved only in this browser.


## Steps

1. Click the user card at the bottom of the sidebar → **Personal Profile**.
2. Select the **Preferences** tab ("Applies only to this browser and is not saved to your account.").
3. Under **AI Assistant**, select **By organization (…)** (uses the administrator's default), **Right panel**, or **Floating bubble**. Your selection takes effect immediately.

## Troubleshooting

- The **AI Assistant** setting isn't visible: the assistant hasn't been enabled for you, or your screen is narrow, such as on a phone (under 768 px; in that case, the assistant always opens in the **Right panel**).
- If you switch browsers or devices, the setting reverts to **By organization**.


## Prerequisites

- [AI Assistant is enabled for your role](../../../troubleshooting/assistant/tools-enabled.md) — An administrator has enabled at least one assistant tool available to your role, so the AI Assistant button appears.

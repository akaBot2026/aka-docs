# Comment on or share a request

Users want to discuss a request or let a colleague view it.

**When to use:**

- Users need to ask the approver or creator about a request.
- Users want a colleague to view a request.


Comment, mention people, and share requests on the request details page.


## Ways to do this

- [Discuss and share a request](how-to/discuss-request.md) — On the request details page, write in the Discussion section or click Share to let others view it.

## Before you start

- **[Who can view a request](../../reference/tracking/visibility.md)**\
  Administrators, operators, creators, assigned approvers or followers, people in the relevant organizational unit tree, and people the request is shared with can view it. Others cannot.

## Steps

[Discuss and share a request](how-to/discuss-request.md)

1. [Discuss and share a request](how-to/discuss-request.md)
   1. Open the details page of the submitted request.
   2. In **Discussion**, enter your comment in the "Write a comment…" field and send it (type @ to mention a person, an organizational unit, or an attachment).
   3. To let others view the request, click **Share** (in the "Share request" dialog): selected users can find the request under **Tracking** → **Following**, open and view it, and receive notifications. Click **Share** to confirm.
   4. View all actions on the **History** tab.

## Troubleshooting

### [Discuss and share a request](how-to/discuss-request.md)

- Draft requests cannot be shared ("Only submitted requests can be shared"). For information about who can view a request, see [Who can view a request](../../reference/tracking/visibility.md).

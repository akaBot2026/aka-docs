# Reject a request

The approver disagrees with and permanently rejects an assigned request.

**When to use:**

- The approver finds an error in the request or notices a missing receipt and wants to reject it.
- The approver wants to permanently reject a request.


The approver decides not to approve the request.


## How to do it

- [Reject a request](how-to/reject-request.md) — Open the request awaiting your action and click Reject on the Your Task card. Enter a reason if you want, then click Confirm. This decision is final.

## Before you begin

- **[Rules for approving, rejecting, returning, and delegating](../../reference/approvals/action-rules.md)**\
  Returning a request requires a reason; delegating requires selecting another recipient; rejecting does not require a reason; approval may require a signature.

## Steps

[Reject a request](how-to/reject-request.md)

1. [Reject a request](how-to/reject-request.md)
   1. Open **Tasks** → the **Pending** tab and click the request to open its details page (or use the ⋯ menu on the row and select **Reject**).
   2. On the "Your Task" card, click **Reject**.
   3. Enter a **Reason** (optional), then click **Confirm**; click **Cancel** to stop.

## Troubleshooting

### [Reject a request](how-to/reject-request.md)

- A rejected request cannot be edited or resubmitted; the creator must create a new one using [Clone a request](../requests/how-to/clone-request.md). See [Rules for approving, rejecting, returning, and delegating](../../reference/approvals/action-rules.md).

# Delegate an Approval Task to Someone Else

A reviewer who is absent or not responsible for an approval wants to transfer the task to someone else.

**When to use:**

- The reviewer wants a colleague to approve on their behalf.
- The reviewer is taking leave and wants to delegate the task.


You need someone else to handle a task assigned to you.


## Ways to do this

- [Delegate a task to someone else](how-to/delegate-task.md) — Open the request, click Delegate, select a recipient, and click Confirm.

## Before you start

- **[Rules for approving, rejecting, returning, and delegating](../../reference/approvals/action-rules.md)**\
  Returning requires a reason; delegating requires selecting another recipient; rejecting does not require a reason; approval may require a signature.

## Steps

[Delegate a task to someone else](how-to/delegate-task.md)

1. [Delegate a task to someone else](how-to/delegate-task.md)
   1. Open **Tasks to Handle** → the **Pending** tab, then click the request to open its details page (or click the ⋯ menu on the row → **Assign / Delegate**).
   2. On the "Your Task" card, click **Delegate**.
   3. Select a person in the **Delegate to** field (required).
   4. Enter a **Reason** if needed (optional; for example, "I'm taking leave this week").
   5. Click **Confirm**.

## If something goes wrong

### [Delegate a task to someone else](how-to/delegate-task.md)

- You can't delegate a task to yourself, the person already assigned to that step, or someone who isn't in the directory. See [Rules for approving, rejecting, returning, and delegating](../../reference/approvals/action-rules.md).

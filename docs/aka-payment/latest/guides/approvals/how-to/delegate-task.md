# Delegate a task to someone else

Open the request, click Delegate, select a recipient, and click Confirm.

## Before you begin

- **[Rules for approving, rejecting, returning, and delegating](../../../reference/approvals/action-rules.md)**\
  Returning a request requires a reason; delegating requires selecting another recipient; rejecting does not require a reason; approving may require a signature.


## Steps

1. Open **Tasks** → the **Pending** tab, and click the request to open its details page (or use the ⋯ menu on the row → **Assign / Delegate**).
2. In the "Your task" card, click **Delegate**.
3. Select a person in the **Delegate to** field (required).
4. Enter a **Reason** if needed (optional; for example, "I'm on leave this week").
5. Click **Confirm**.

## Troubleshooting

- Delegation is rejected if you delegate to yourself, someone already assigned to that step, or someone who is not in the directory. See [Rules for approving, rejecting, returning, and delegating](../../../reference/approvals/action-rules.md).

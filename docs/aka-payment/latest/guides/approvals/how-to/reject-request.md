# Reject a request

Open the request awaiting your action and click Reject on the Your Task card. Enter a reason if you want, then click Confirm. This decision is final.

## Before you start

- **[Rules for approving, rejecting, returning, and delegating](../../../reference/approvals/action-rules.md)**\
  Returning requires a reason; delegation requires selecting another recipient; rejection does not require a reason; approval may require a signature.


## Steps

1. Open **Tasks to Handle** → the **Pending** tab, then click the request to open its details page (or use the ⋯ menu on the row and select **Reject**).
2. On the "Your Task" card, click **Reject**.
3. Enter a **Reason** (optional), then click **Confirm**; click **Cancel** to go back.

## Troubleshooting

- A rejected request cannot be edited or resubmitted; the creator must create a new one using [Clone a request](../../requests/how-to/clone-request.md). See [Rules for approving, rejecting, returning, and delegating](../../../reference/approvals/action-rules.md).

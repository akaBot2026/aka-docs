# Return a Request to Its Creator

Open the request, click Return, enter the required reason, and click Confirm. The creator can then edit and resubmit it.

## Before you begin

- **[Rules for approving, rejecting, returning, and delegating](../../../reference/approvals/action-rules.md)**\
  Returning requires a reason; delegation requires selecting a different recipient; rejection does not require a reason; approval may require a signature.


## Steps

1. Open **Action Items** → the **Pending** tab, then click the request to open its details page.
2. On the "Your Task" card, click **Return** (this button is available only on the details page, not in the list's ⋯ menu).
3. Enter a **Reason** (required; the field's placeholder is: "Please attach the signed contract").
4. Click **Confirm**. The creator can continue with [Edit and resubmit a returned request](../../requests/how-to/resubmit-returned.md).

## Troubleshooting

- The Confirm button is disabled: You haven't entered a reason. See [Rules for approving, rejecting, returning, and delegating](../../../reference/approvals/action-rules.md).

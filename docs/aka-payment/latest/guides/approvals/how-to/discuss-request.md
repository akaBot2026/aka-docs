# Discuss and share a request

On the request details page, post in Discussion or click Share to let others view it.

## Before you begin

- **[Who can view a request](../../../reference/tracking/visibility.md)**\
  Administrators, operators, the creator, assigned approvers or followers, people in the relevant organizational unit hierarchy, and people the request is shared with can view it; others cannot.


## Steps

1. Open the details page of the submitted request.
2. In **Discussion**, enter your message in the "Write a comment…" field and send it (type @ to mention a person, an organizational unit, or an attachment).
3. To let others view the request, click **Share** (in the "Share request" dialog): selected people can find the request under **Follow** → **Following**, open and view it, and receive a notification; click **Share** to confirm.
4. View all actions in the **History** tab.

## Troubleshooting

- Draft requests cannot be shared ("Only submitted requests can be shared"). For details about who can view a request, see [Who can view a request](../../../reference/tracking/visibility.md).

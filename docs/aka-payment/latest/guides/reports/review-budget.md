# Compare budget with actual spending

Users want to compare the budget plan with actual spending.

**When to use:** Users want to know how much they have spent compared with their budget.


Budget Plan vs. Actual report.


## Ways to do this

- [Open the Budget Plan vs. Actual report](how-to/review-budget.md) — In the sidebar, open Reports → Budget Plan vs. Actual, select a month range, and review the data by unit, budget item, or details.

## Steps

[Open the Budget Plan vs. Actual report](how-to/review-budget.md)

1. [Open the Budget Plan vs. Actual report](how-to/review-budget.md)
   1. In the sidebar, open **Reports** (1).
      ![Budget Plan vs. Actual report: (1) Reports group in the sidebar, (2) Budget Plan vs. Actual item, (3) By Cost Center tab next to By Budget Item and Detailed Report, (4) month selector next to the Budget Item field; table with Opening balance, Used, In progress, Total budget, Cumulative total budget, Utilization %](/static/img/aka-payment-latest-reports-budget-vs-actual.webp)
   2. Select **Budget Plan vs. Actual** (2) (the "Budget Plan vs. Actual report" page).
   3. Switch between the **By Cost Center** (3), **By Budget Item**, and **Detailed Report** tabs.
   4. Select a month range using the month selector (4) (the current month is selected by default; up to 24 months are allowed. If the range is invalid, the message "Select a range of up to 24 months, with the start month no later than the end month." appears). Filter by **Budget Item** (By Cost Center and Detailed Report tabs) or **Organizational Unit** (By Budget Item and Detailed Report tabs), if needed.
   5. Review the columns: Opening balance, Used, In progress, Total budget, Cumulative total budget, and Utilization %. Use the download icon (**Export Excel**) to export the data, and the refresh icon to reload it. The report is only available on desktop.

# Open the Budget Plan vs. Actual report

In the sidebar, open Reports → Budget Plan vs. Actual, select a month range, and view the data by unit, budget item, or in detail.


## Steps

1. In the sidebar, open **Reports** (1).

   ![Budget Plan vs. Actual report: (1) Reports group in the sidebar, (2) Budget Plan vs. Actual item, (3) By Cost Center tab next to By Budget Item and Detailed Report, (4) month selector next to the Budget Item field; columns Opening Balance, Spent, In Progress, Total Budget, Cumulative Total Budget, Usage %](/static/img/aka-payment-latest-reports-budget-vs-actual.webp)

2. Select **Budget Plan vs. Actual** (2) (the "Budget Plan vs. Actual report" page).
3. Switch between the **By Cost Center** (3), **By Budget Item**, and **Detailed Report** tabs.
4. Select a month range in the month selector (4) (defaults to the current month; up to 24 months. If the range is invalid, "Select a range of up to 24 months, with the start month no later than the end month." appears). Filter by **Budget Item** (By Cost Center and Detailed Report tabs) or **Organization Unit** (By Budget Item and Detailed Report tabs), if needed.
5. Review the columns: Opening Balance, Spent, In Progress, Total Budget, Cumulative Total Budget, and Usage %. Use the download icon (**Export to Excel**) to export, and the refresh icon to reload. The report is only available on desktop.
